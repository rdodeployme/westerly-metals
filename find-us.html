Questions

Before you head over.

Do I need to book?

Not for a ute, trailer or small truck. Just come in between 6am and 6pm. For a big truck, machinery or a full clean-up, call ahead so we're ready.

Do you pay for scrap metal?

It depends on the metal and the load. Clean, sorted copper, brass, aluminium and stainless are worth the most. Mixed steel is worth less, and some items carry a handling fee. Call Sean with what you've got for today's rates.

Why can't you pay cash?

Victorian law has banned cash payments for scrap metal since May 2018. Any payment is by bank transfer, and we have to record your name, address and ID. Bring your licence.

Is it really closer than Melbourne?

From most of the Western District it's about 45 km closer each way than the Laverton North yards, so around 90 km less per round trip. Hamilton and the far west save about 70 km. Check your town.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, chemicals and hazardous waste, full or hired gas cylinders, anything still holding fuel or oil, and general rubbish. See the full list.

Fridges, air-cons, car bodies?

Call first. Some items need gas removed or fluids drained before they come in, and we'll tell you how.

Can you pick up from my farm or business?

Yes, across the Western District. Every pickup is quoted before we come. Ask about a pickup.
