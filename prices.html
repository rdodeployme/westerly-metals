    What sets the price

    Four things move the number.

    The same tonne of metal can be worth very different money. Here's what makes the difference.

    01

    The metal

    Copper and brass pay the most per kilo, then aluminium, stainless and lead. Steel pays the least but adds up by the tonne.

    02

    The grade

    Bright copper pays more than cable. Clean extrusion pays more than mixed aluminium. Sorting by grade puts money in your pocket.

    03

    How clean it is

    Steel bolted to brass, plastic on cable or oil in a motor brings the rate down to the lowest grade in the pile.

    04

    Handling

    Fridges and air-cons need their gas removed, so they carry a handling fee. Car bodies and part-metal items are paid at a lower rate.

    Getting paid

    Bank transfer, on the spot.

    Victorian law has banned cash for scrap metal since May 2018. We weigh your metal, grade it in front of you and pay by bank transfer. Bring photo ID and your bank details.
