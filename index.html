Open when you are

Before work. After knock-off. All weekend.

Most scrap yards keep office hours and shut for the weekend. We open the gate at 6am and close it at 6pm, every day of the week.

What we take

If it's metal, bring it in.

Farm scrap, trade offcuts and household metal. Sort the good stuff from the steel and it's worth more. Anything unusual, call first.

Farm scrap

Fencing wire, star pickets, gates, troughs, corrugated iron, old implements and machinery.

What farms bring

Trade offcuts

Steel, copper pipe, cable, aluminium frames, reo, roofing and gutter from the job.

What trades bring

Household metal

BBQs with the bottle off, bikes, trampolines, filing cabinets and drained mowers.

What households bring

Sorted metals

Copper, brass, aluminium, stainless and lead. Keep them apart from the steel and ask about rates.

Metals we sort

How it works

In, weighed, unloaded, gone.

A ute or trailer doesn't need a booking. Bringing a truck, machinery or a big clean-up? Call ahead so we're ready for you.

01

Load up

Keep copper, brass and aluminium apart from the steel if you can. Drain fuel and oil, and take gas bottles off BBQs.

02

Drive in

Any day, 6am to 6pm. Big loads or trucks, give us a call first.

03

Weigh and unload

Weigh in, unload where the yard team points you, weigh out.

04

Settle up

Rates depend on the metal and the load. Any payment is by bank transfer, so bring photo ID.

What it pays, what it costs

Depends on the load.

Clean, sorted copper, brass, aluminium and stainless are worth the most. Mixed steel is worth less, and some items carry a handling fee. Call ahead with what you've got and we'll tell you where it sits.

Victorian law bans cash for scrap metal, so any payment is by bank transfer and we record your name, address and ID.
