We'll come to you.

Bins and pickups for farms, workshops, builders and businesses right across the Western District. Tell us what you've got and where, and we'll quote it before we come.

Who we pick up from

One-off clean-ups or regular loads.

If it's more than a ute can carry, or it keeps piling up, a pickup is usually the cheaper way to move it.

Farm clean-ups

Old fencing, implements, machinery, sheds and the pile behind the shearing shed. We bring the bin or the truck to the paddock.

Workshops and trades

A bin on site for offcuts, swapped when it's full. For engineering shops, fabricators, plumbers, electricians and builders.

Businesses and councils

Depots, transfer stations, dealers and contractors with steady volumes. Regular runs, booked around you.
